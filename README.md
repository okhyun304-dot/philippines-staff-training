# Philippines Staff Training

Two bilingual pork-belly cooking training materials for kitchen staff.

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [training.html](training.html) | Six-step quick reference for use during work. |
| [handbook.html](handbook.html) | Detailed reading: reasons, methods, photo observations, 12 scenarios, 6 review questions and supervised practice. |

Download the HTML files and open them in Chrome or Edge. Both start in English and support Korean. Photos and short silent video clips are included in the files. Tap a main photo to play the clip in place. No video download or external video player is needed during use.

The handbook includes a contents menu, text-size controls and store-target settings. A trainer can enter confirmed targets and save a shareable HTML file that includes them.

## Training status

These are working training drafts. Store targets for meat thickness, seasoning, grill preheat, batch size, portion size, doneness and rest have not been set. The trainer should review the material against the store process and supervise practice before independent work.
